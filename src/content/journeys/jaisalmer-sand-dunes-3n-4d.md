Jaisalmer is a living sandstone fort in the Thar Desert. Combine it with a
night in desert tents and you have the Rajasthan of the imagination: dunes,
camels, folk music and clear night skies. Jaisalmer has no direct flights from
most Indian cities, so the programme uses Jodhpur as the gateway.
