The shortest way to see the most famous building in India. You fly into Delhi,
drive to Agra and wake early for the Taj Mahal at first light, when the white
marble turns from grey to pink to gold and the crowds are thinnest. It is a
compact programme, but it is paced so that the Taj gets your best hours of the
day.
