Step into a land where Mughal emperors built monuments to love, maharajas ruled
from pink sandstone palaces, and leopards still prowl the scrubland on the edge
of a royal capital. This is the classic Golden Triangle — Delhi, Agra and Jaipur
— with one addition that changes the character of the week: a full day at the
Jhalana Leopard Reserve, a rocky forest inside Jaipur's city limits that has
one of the highest leopard densities anywhere in India.

Seven days, three cities, and a single base in Jaipur for the last three
nights, so the safaris come at the end of the trip rather than as a detour from
it. It is history, heritage and a genuine wildlife encounter in one week, and
an ideal first introduction to the country.
