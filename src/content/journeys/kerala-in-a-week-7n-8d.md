Some places you visit; Kerala you sink into. This is the one-week version of
that, and it is built on a simple idea: fly into the north and out of the
south, so that the journey runs in one direction — Fort Kochi's old streets,
the tea country above Munnar, the spice hills at Thekkady, a night on the
backwaters at Alappuzha, and a beach at the end — with no long doubling back.

It is the Kerala we recommend to first-time visitors and to anyone with only a
week. The longer journeys we run here add a Kodaikanal day and three nights on
the Mararikulam sand, or start in the far south and reach Kanyakumari; this one
takes the essentials in the right order and stops while you still want more.
