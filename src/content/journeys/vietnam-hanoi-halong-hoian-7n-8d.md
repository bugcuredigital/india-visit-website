Vietnam runs more than a thousand miles from north to south, and the useful
first visit does not try to cover it. This eight-day journey takes the north
and the centre — Hanoi's Old Quarter and its food, a night on the water among
the limestone karsts of Ha Long Bay, and then a short flight to Da Nang for the
beach and the lantern-lit old town of Hoi An — and leaves the south for another
time.

It is built for first-time travellers: one internal flight, one night afloat, a
street-food walk in place of a restaurant list, and a last day you choose
between a mountaintop spectacle and a quiet cave. The visa is an e-visa, the
currency is cash-friendly, and every transfer, cruise and guide is arranged.
