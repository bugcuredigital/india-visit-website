Kabini sits on the banks of a reservoir on the edge of Nagarhole National
Park, in the forests of the Nilgiri Biosphere. It is known for elephants
gathering at the water, for leopards and for the chance of a tiger. The
programme is built around two safaris a day, with your closest airport at
Mysuru, so almost no time is lost on the road.
