Tadoba is the quick answer to a question we are asked often: is there a tiger
reserve that can be done properly in a long weekend? Three hours from Nagpur,
in the geographical centre of the country, it is Maharashtra's oldest and
largest national park and one of the best tiger habitats in India — teak
forest, bamboo groves and a string of lakes, in the kind of landscape often
said to have inspired *The Jungle Book*.

This short version is built for first-time wildlife travellers and for anyone
whose dates will not stretch: two nights at a jungle resort on the park's edge,
three safaris with a naturalist, and enough time between them to sit still. It
combines easily with the rest of central India, and we build it into longer
journeys as often as we sell it on its own.
