Two very different sacred worlds in one trip. Varanasi, one of the oldest
living cities on earth, is all river, ritual and sound. Khajuraho, a few hours
later, is quiet stone: temples of the Chandela dynasty covered in sculpture.
Together they show how deeply art and belief are woven together in India.
