The Golden Triangle is the best week-long introduction to northern India. Add
Varanasi and it becomes something else: a journey that reaches the Ganges, one
of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world, and the spiritual
centre of Hinduism — and gets there by train, which is how the country still
mostly travels.

Ten days, then: Delhi, Lucknow's Nawabi courtliness, two nights and a dawn boat
ride in Varanasi, and then Agra and Jaipur on the way back. Two of the nights
are spent on the railway, in reserved air-conditioned berths, and travellers
who were unsure about that tend to name it afterwards as the part they enjoyed
most. If you would rather fly either leg, we will arrange it.
