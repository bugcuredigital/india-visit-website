Three cities, five hundred years, and one of the most travelled routes in the
world — which is exactly why it is worth doing properly. The Golden Triangle
earned its name because Delhi, Agra and Jaipur sit almost equidistant from one
another, and because between them they hold the Mughal and Rajput high-water
marks: a capital rebuilt seven times, a city drawn on paper before a stone was
laid, and a tomb that has never been bettered.

This version takes six days rather than four. That extra room is the whole
point: sunrise at the Taj instead of mid-morning, an afternoon free in Jaipur's
bazaars, and Fatehpur Sikri treated as a destination rather than a photo stop on
the way to somewhere else. Every transfer, guide and reservation is handled, so
the only decision left to you is when to stop for chai.
