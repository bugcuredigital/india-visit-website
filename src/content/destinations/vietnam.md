Vietnam is long, and that single fact shapes every itinerary. The north and the
south sit in different climate zones, cook differently and feel different, and
the useful route treats the country as three regions joined by short flights
rather than as one place to be driven through.

We generally build it as Hanoi and an overnight on the water in the north, Hue
or Hoi An in the centre, and Ho Chi Minh City with a Mekong day in the south.
The middle section is the one travellers most often want to extend — Hoi An's
old town, the tailors, and the beach an hour away make a good argument for an
extra two nights.
